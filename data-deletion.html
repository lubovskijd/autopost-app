Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Autopost stores a minimal amount of data: access tokens for the owner's own accounts and, when a person comments with the keyword under the owner's posts, the comment ID, the commenter's public username, the comment text and the reply that was sent. People who start the owner's Telegram bot may also leave answers to a short questionnaire.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a message to the Telegram bot @lubouski_leads_bot or a direct message to the Instagram or Facebook account where you left the comment, with the words "delete my data".
  2. Within 30 days all records linked to your username or Telegram account are deleted from the Autopost database, and you receive a confirmation in the same chat.

Removing the app's access

If you connected an account to Autopost, you can remove its access at any time: Facebook and Instagram in Settings → Business integrations or in the Instagram app under Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites; Google in Google Account permissions; TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings → Security and permissions.
